§ 1 The parties

Trained in law. Built in business.

I qualified as a solicitor in England and Wales after studying Law and Criminology, completing the LPC and working in legal practice.

In 2015 I moved into tech recruitment as head of legal at a recruitment business. In 2018 I joined a new organisation to broaden my experience, and in 2020 I founded Alium Consultancy. Alongside the business I mentor students, serve as a school governor and speak at universities in England, Portugal and the USA.

§ 2 Scope of work

Four things I do.

  • Build

    Founder & CEO · Alium Consultancy

    A tech, data and health staffing business, run remotely across the globe from offices in London, Amsterdam and Delaware.

  • Advise

    Universities · Schools · Broadcast
