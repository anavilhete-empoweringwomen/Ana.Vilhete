Why do I still feel stuck when everything looks successful?

By Ana Vilhete · 26 November 2026

Because feeling stuck doesn't always look like standing still. For me it looked like the opposite. I was busy all the time and achieving things, but my inner sense of peace wasn't moving at all.

I spoke about this with Black Ballad, and the response showed me how many women recognise it.

What it felt like

I started therapy in 2021. I was studying for my Executive MBA, growing my businesses and raising my family through Covid. I wasn't in crisis. I went because I wanted to grow.

I did the work: therapy, journalling, long conversations with mentors and friends who are high achievers themselves. And still I felt stuck. The best description I've found is highly functional exhaustion.

It's like running a marathon on a treadmill.

From the outside, everything was moving. Inside, I was in the same place.

Why it's common for women like me

Many high-achieving women, and many Black women in particular, learn early that we have to be twice as prepared and never drop the ball. That habit builds careers. It also makes it very easy to keep going long after you should have stopped to ask whether you're okay.

If you recognise yourself here, being productive doesn't mean you're fine. It's worth saying that to someone you trust, or to a professional.

What helps me

I don't have a neat answer, and I'm wary of anyone who does. But one thing reliably makes a difference: moving my body.

I used to train every day before I had children. Now, with three, it's often once a week, but I've found my own way of keeping fit around a busy life. I walk at lunchtime. At weekends my family looks after the children while I go to a spin class with friends. When I can't get to the gym, I put on a YouTube workout at home. I told Stylist that I manage stress at home much better after I've worked out, and that's still true.

  • Pick the version of exercise you'll actually do, not the one you think you should do.
  • Ask for help with childcare so you can have that hour, and accept it when it's offered.
  • Notice when being busy has become a way of not checking in with yourself.

If you're struggling, speak to your GP or contact Mind (mind.org.uk) or Samaritans (116 123, free, any time).

Quick answers
What does 'highly functional exhaustion' mean?

It's how Ana Vilhete describes feeling stuck while still achieving: constant work and visible success on the outside, while your inner sense of peace doesn't move.

How does Ana Vilhete manage stress?

She keeps exercise in her week even when it's only once: lunchtime walks, a weekend spin class with friends and YouTube workouts at home. She says she manages stress at home much better after she has worked out.
