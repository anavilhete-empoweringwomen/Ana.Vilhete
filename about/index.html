Biography

From Luanda to London

Ana Vilhete was born in Luanda during Angola's civil war. Her family moved to Lisbon when she was a young child, and at 16 she moved to London, where she learned English, completed her A-level studies and went on to study Law and Criminology. She has spoken openly about discovering she is dyslexic at law school, and about growing up without the safety nets many founders take for granted.

After completing the Legal Practice Course at BPP Law School and working in legal practice, she qualified as a solicitor in England and Wales. In 2015 she moved into technology recruitment as head of legal at a recruitment business, and in 2018 joined a new organisation to broaden her leadership experience.

In 2020 she founded Alium Consultancy, a staffing business placing talent in technology, data and healthcare roles, with a focus on diversity in the workforce and a remote-first way of working. In April 2024 she co-founded Themis Crown Advocates, a London law firm practising immigration, employment, commercial and charity law. She completed an Executive MBA at Warwick Business School in 2022.

As one of a small number of Black women leading both a staffing business and a law firm in the UK, Ana uses her platform to open doors for others. Her message to students and young founders is simple: if she could do it, so can they.
